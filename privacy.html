Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Demo project. NOVA Home is a portfolio demonstration. Please do not enter real personal, payment or sensitive information. Demo accounts and their data may be reset.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what the NOVA Home demo stores when you browse, create an account, or try checkout, and the choices you have.

2. Information you provide

  • Account details: your name, email address and Egyptian mobile number when you register.
  • Order details: the items, quantities, delivery city and any note you enter during the demo checkout.
  • Saved addresses: entries you add to the account area.

3. Information stored in your browser

Cart and wishlist contents are kept in your browser’s localStorage (keys such as nova_cart, nova_wish and nova_delivery_city) so they survive page reloads. Clearing site data removes them.

4. Cookies and sessions

A PHP session cookie keeps you signed in. It is required for account and checkout features and is not used for advertising. The demo does not run third-party analytics.

5. How information is used

  • To sign you in and show your account, wishlist and demo orders.
  • To calculate cart totals and display a delivery estimate.
  • To keep the demo secure and diagnose errors.

6. Storage and security

Passwords are stored using PHP's password-hashing API, never as plain text. Sessions are server-side. Registration checks that you accept the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy before creating an account; this demo does not retain a separate consent timestamp or policy-version record. No real card or bank details are requested or stored. Despite these measures, a demonstration deployment should not be treated as a secure place for real personal data.

7. Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and CSS/JavaScript from the jsDelivr CDN. Those providers receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser details. No advertising or analytics scripts are included.

8. Sharing

Demo data is not sold or shared for marketing. Because this is a demonstration, other visitors to a shared public demo may see or change shared sample records; do not enter anything private.

9. Retention and your choices

Demo records may be reset or deleted at any time. You can clear cart and wishlist data by clearing your browser’s site data. There is no self-service account-deletion feature or live support contact in this demo; the footer phone number and email address are placeholders and must not be relied on for deletion requests. Do not register with personal information you would need kept private or removed.

10. Children

The demo is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 16 to submit personal information.

11. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the project changes; the date at the top shows the latest revision. Contact details in the footer are sample placeholders for this demonstration.
